# Third-party components and benchmark data

This repository contains **no third-party source code** and **no third-party
network files**. Every benchmark network in the table of section 2 is fetched
at setup time by `scripts/fetch_benchmarks.py` (remote downloads) or read from
your local `wntr` installation, then verified against a SHA-256 manifest.
Licence strings are quoted from the upstream repository or record as of the
audit date below; where an upstream repository makes **no licence statement**,
that is recorded verbatim as "not stated": the file is *not* redistributed
here and you must satisfy yourself that your use is permitted.

The network files that *are* in this repository are the authors' own: the 23
synthetic networks (section 4) and the two anonymised operational models with
their leak work-order table (section 5).

Audit date: 2026-08-09. Source of the inventory: `data/public_inventory.json`
(generated by `scripts/inventory_public.py`). All URLs below were re-checked
with an HTTP GET on 2026-08-09 and returned HTTP 200 with a byte count equal
to the `file_size` recorded in `data/public_inventory.json`.

difference from the Zenodo file is the removal of one `[OPTIONS]` line
(`Pattern 1`, the default-pattern pointer, which the parser treats
identically) and an LF-to-CRLF line-ending change. Both hashes are recorded so
the bytes can be checked (`data/mainline_evidence.md`, section 0):

### Networks with "not stated" licence

For the 9 rows marked **not stated**, the upstream GitHub repository
(`KIOS-Research/EPANET-Benchmarks`) carries no `LICENSE` file. These files are
therefore never redistributed by this project. `fetch_benchmarks.py` prints an
explicit warning for each of them:

```
[LICENCE] not stated upstream - academic use; verify permissions yourself.
```

If you need a licence-clean subset, run
`python scripts/fetch_benchmarks.py --only-licensed`, which restricts the
download to the CC-BY-4.0 / MIT / BSD-3 rows.

## 3. Data that is deliberately absent

The following inputs are used in the authors' internal experiments and are
**excluded from this repository and from every published artefact** because
they are operational data belonging to a water utility:

- the un-anonymised City D distribution network model plus the associated
  spreadsheets of demands, asset checks and burst records;
- the un-anonymised utility leak/work-order records;
- the un-anonymised City H model.

Anonymised, hydraulically identical releases of the City D and City H models
and of the leak work-order table *are* published, under `datasets/`
(CC BY 4.0); see `datasets/README.md`. What remains absent is the
un-anonymised source form of those same inputs (real projected coordinates,
street addresses, metering-district names and absolute repair dates).

Also absent: the `EXA4`, `EXA5`, `EXA6`, `ky3` and `ky5` models that appear as
rows of `data/benchmark_report.txt` and `data/regression_report.txt`. `EXA4`
to `EXA6` are EPANET example models from a locally held collection with no
citable source; `ky3` and `ky5` are University of Kentucky WDST models whose
local copies are not part of the fetch manifest. The rows that use them are
reproducible only where those files are present (the `ky4` and `ky10` models
that *are* fetched come from the same Kentucky collection through WNTR).
Aggregate, non-reconstructive measurements taken on all of
these networks (timings, error norms) are reported in the README and in
`data/*.txt`; the network topologies, demands and records themselves are not
distributed and cannot be reconstructed from those numbers.

## 4. Synthetic networks shipped here

`networks/random_main/rand_0000..0019.inp` and
`networks/random_small/rand_0000..0002.inp` (23 files) were generated by the
authors with `networks/synthetic/generate_random_networks.py` (seeds 20260803
and 11) through the EPANET 2.2 toolkit. They contain no utility data and no
third-party content; node ids are `J1..Jn` / `R1..R2`. They are released under
this repository's MIT licence, and their SHA-256 values are recorded in
`networks/synthetic/SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## 5. Datasets shipped here

`datasets/city_d.inp`, `datasets/city_h.inp` and

byte-identical copy of `datasets/city_d.inp` kept next to the self-contained
